### Interpretation

* **Flood-Focused** prioritises flood storage, stakeholder acceptance and minimising disservices.
* **Carbon-Focused** prioritises long-term carbon sequestration benefits.
* **Habitat-Focused** prioritises biodiversity and ecosystem enhancement.
* **Cost-Focused** prioritises affordability and cost-effectiveness.
* **Policy-Focused** prioritises alignment with strategic and regulatory objectives.
* **Land Owner Perspective** prioritises practical implementation, cost considerations and delivery risks.

For multi-benefit decision-making, the custom AHP workflow is recommended, allowing stakeholders to define project-specific priorities rather than relying solely on predefined perspectives.
